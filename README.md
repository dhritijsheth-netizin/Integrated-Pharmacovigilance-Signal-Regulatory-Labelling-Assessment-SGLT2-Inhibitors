This repository contains an end-to-end pharmacovigilance evaluation investigating the safety signal of Fournier's gangrene associated with Sodium-Glucose Cotransporter-2 (SGLT2) inhibitors (empagliflozin, dapagliflozin, and canagliflozin). The project bridges quantitative disproportionality analysis, individual case safety report processing, and regulatory label auditing.   Note: Individual case narratives included in this project are simulated training data created solely for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes.
Project Workflow & SequenceQuantitative Signal DetectionAnalyzes aggregate FAERS disproportionality reporting data.   Calculates the Proportional Reporting Ratio (PRR ~ 7.95) and applies Chi-square statistical testing.   Case Narrative IntakeProvides raw clinical narratives for 5 simulated individual case safety reports (FG-001 through FG-005) presenting with Fournier's gangrene.   Case Processing & EvaluationTranslates raw source narratives into structured safety data.Evaluates minimum validity criteria, seriousness criteria (hospitalization, surgical intervention, fatal outcomes), and applies WHO-UMC causality classifications.Medical & Epidemiological SynthesisSynthesizes epidemiological literature (Fadini et al., 2019) and the 2018 FDA Drug Safety Communication alongside biological plausibility and clinical risk factors (diabetes mellitus, obesity).   Regulatory Audit & DecisionConcludes the workflow by auditing existing US Prescribing Information for Jardiance (empagliflozin), confirming that Section 5.3 (Genitourinary Infections) appropriately addresses the safety warning without requiring immediate label modifications.   Key Methodologies & FrameworksSignal Detection: Proportional Reporting Ratio (PRR) & Chi-Square Analysis.   Causality Assessment: WHO-UMC Causality Classification Scale.Regulatory Compliance: US FDA Prescribing Guidelines & ICH-GCP Post-Marketing Safety Standards.
